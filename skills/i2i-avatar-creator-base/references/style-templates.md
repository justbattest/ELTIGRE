# Style Templates — A through E

Five styles cover all the use cases for avatar prompts. Style A is the default and covers ~90% of requests. The other styles are for specific looks.

**Critical**: Never mix styles. If you choose Style A (UGC iPhone smartphone), don't add Style C (cinematic 50mm) vocabulary. Each style has its own coherent visual logic.

---

## Style A — UGC iPhone Smartphone (DEFAULT — 90% of prompts)

### When to use
- Any request that doesn't specify a "professional photo" feel
- Selfie / mirror selfie / casual smartphone candid
- Lifestyle moment, gym, café, car, store, hotel room
- "A friend just took my photo" energy
- Influencer UGC content

### Camera specs
- iPhone 15 Pro front camera (selfie, mirror selfie)
- iPhone 15 Pro rear camera (taken by a friend, third-person, mirror full-body)
- 24mm equivalent for ultra-wide selfie / 26mm for normal smartphone / 28mm for slightly tighter
- Eye-level OR slightly below eye-level OR slightly above (no extreme angles)
- Arm-length distance for selfie / handheld for third-person
- NO flash (unless using Style D specifically)

### Lighting
- Natural light (window, daylight, golden hour) preferred
- Soft warm interior LED OK (cove lighting, recessed ceiling lights)
- Fluorescent (gym, store, gas station) OK for grittier look
- AVOID: studio lighting, ring light glow (unless visible in frame as a prop), softbox, dramatic rim lighting

### Color grade
- Natural / iPhone default rendering
- Warm cream highlights, soft falloff, mild iPhone HDR
- Slight halation around bright sources OK
- Slight iPhone over-sharpening at edges OK

### Mandatory inclusions
- Mid-action pose
- Specific location + city + time
- Lived-in imperfections (3+)
- Brand-named outfit + jewelry
- 5+ specific background objects
- Identity preservation phrase

## Style B — Podcast / Interview Studio

### When to use
- Podcast appearance scene
- Interview / conversation setting
- "She's being filmed talking" moments
- Joe Rogan / Lex Fridman / Diary of a CEO aesthetic

### Camera specs
- Sony A7R IV / Canon R5 / "shot on a full-frame mirrorless camera"
- 50mm or 85mm lens
- f/2.0 to f/2.8 (medium-shallow depth of field)
- Eye-level
- Mid-shot framing (chest-up to waist-up)
- Multi-camera shoot vibe (slight off-axis angles work)

### Lighting
- Warm key light from one side
- Soft fill light or natural bounce
- Subtle rim light on hair
- Background slightly dimmer than subject
- "Practical lights" visible in background OK (warm bulbs, neon signs, edison bulbs)

### Color grade
- Cinematic warm — warm tungsten highlights, deep amber shadows, rich midtones
- Slight contrast push
- Natural film-like color, not Instagram-filtered

### Visual signature
- Podcast mic on boom arm, partly in frame
- Acoustic foam panels OR wood paneling background
- Warm wood / leather / brass tones
- Calm, conversational expression
- Professional but candid (caught mid-thought, not posed)

### Template

Preserve the exact identity from the reference image: same face structure, same eye shape and color, same eyebrows, same nose shape, same lip shape, same skin tone and texture, same hair color and texture, same body proportions. Do not smooth the face. Do not soften the features.

She is mid-sentence, mouth slightly open in expressive natural conversation, eyes focused off-camera at the host, head tilted very slightly. One hand resting on the table, the other gesturing subtly. Calm focused expression, engaged but unposed.

Negative: no cartoon, no CGI, no plastic skin, no airbrushing, no smoothing, no Instagram filter, no studio backdrop look, no fake bokeh, no warped anatomy, no symmetrical perfection.

## Style C — Cinematic Editorial (Magazine / Lifestyle Pro)

### When to use
- "Behind-the-scenes of a fashion shoot" energy
- Magazine editorial moment
- Outdoor café golden hour close-up
- Pro photographer captured this, not a phone

### Camera specs
- Full-frame mirrorless (Sony A7R / Canon R5)
- 50mm or 85mm prime lens
- Wide aperture f/1.8 to f/2.2
- Ultra-shallow depth of field
- Tack-sharp focus on eyes, dreamy bokeh background

### Lighting
- Pure natural sunlight — golden hour or open shade
- Directional (one side of the face brighter)
- Soft shadow falloff on the opposite side
- Real-world bounce, no artificial fill

### Color grade
- Warm, editorial, slightly golden
- Realistic skin tones
- Subtle contrast, no HDR, no oversharpening
- Sony A7R or iPhone Pro-style "lifestyle photo" rendering

### Visual signature
- Profile / 3/4 angle close-up
- Cropped tight (forehead just out of frame, lips just below)
- Flyaway hairs catching the light
- Eyes glossy, naturally lit
- Slight skin shine in highlight zones

### Template

```

## Style D — Flash Night Photo (Chaotic Energy)

### When to use
- Night / late-evening scene
- Party / event / spontaneous moment
- "Drunk friend snapped a photo without warning" energy
- Gas station / club / parking lot / car at night
- Unplanned, intimate, slightly off-kilter

### Camera specs
- iPhone 15 Pro with flash ON
- 24mm or 26mm equivalent
- Slightly crooked framing (unposed)
- Slight motion blur OK
- Imperfect focus OK

### Lighting
- Hard direct iPhone flash from camera position
- Blown-out highlights on skin, jewelry, reflective surfaces
- Deep shadows everywhere else
- Background fades into darkness beyond flash falloff
- Available ambient light barely visible (gas station tubes, neon, taillights)

### Color grade
- High contrast
- Cool flash highlights / deep magenta or green shadows
- Halation around brightest highlights
- Slight color shift in darker areas
- Gritty, raw, unprocessed

### Visual signature
- Subject mid-laugh, mid-turn, mid-blink
- One feature blown out (skin highlight on cheek, reflective fabric, metal jewelry)
- Shadow falling sharp behind her
- Background mostly dark with one or two glowing elements
- Asphalt, concrete, or fluorescent floor texture sharp under flash

### Template

```
Ultra-realistic raw night-time iPhone flash photo of the same woman shown in the reference image, framing slightly crooked like a rushed snap, taken in [SPECIFIC NIGHT LOCATION — e.g., a gas station forecourt in Miami at 11:47pm / a club bathroom at 1:15am / a parking lot outside a steakhouse at 10:30pm].

Preserve the exact identity from the reference image: same face structure, same eye shape and color, same eyebrows, same nose, same lip shape, same skin tone and texture, same hair color and texture, same body proportions. Do not smooth the face. Do not soften the features.

She is caught mid-[laugh / turn / blink / sip], [BODY ANGLE — slightly turned to the side, hair flying / one hand near her mouth / mid-step]. Her face is [slightly motion-blurred / sharp but caught off-guard], imperfect focus, chaotic energy.

Wearing [OUTFIT — e.g., a satin red strapless mini dress / a fitted black leather tank with low-rise jeans / a slip dress and thin heels].

Behind her: [SPECIFIC NIGHT SETTING — e.g., a black Mustang parked at the pump with taillights glowing red / a row of energy-drink fridges glowing green-white / a strip-mall storefront with a buzzing neon Open sign / cars blurred in the dark distance]. Most of the background fades into shadow beyond the flash falloff, only the brightest elements glowing.

Shot on iPhone 15 Pro rear camera with flash ON, 24mm equivalent, eye-level, slight motion shake. The flash blows out highlights on her skin, the satin fabric, and reflective surfaces. Hard shadows fall sharply behind and to the side of the subject. Gritty, sharp concrete or asphalt or floor texture under the flash.

Skin: harsh flash-blown highlights on the forehead, nose bridge, and cheekbones, deep shadow under the jaw, slight oily skin, [3-5 LIVED-IN IMPERFECTIONS — mascara slightly smudged, faint sheen of sweat, lipstick slightly off-line, a small healing pimple visible under the harsh light]. Natural skin texture fully preserved.

Color grade: cool flash-white highlights, deep magenta shadows, slight halation around the brightest highlight on the satin/skin/metal, raw unprocessed iPhone rendering, gritty contrast, no HDR smoothing.

Negative: no cartoon, no CGI, no plastic skin, no clean studio look, no professional retouch, no symmetric framing, no Instagram filter, no fake bokeh, no airbrushed skin, no posed expression.
```

## Style E — Hyper-Specific Lifestyle (The Most Powerful)

### When to use
- When you want the absolute best result
- A "real moment" of her life with full biographical context
- Combines strongest elements of Style A with maximal narrative specificity
- Use this when the user wants premium output

### Why it works
This is essentially Style A on steroids. Every block is filled with the highest density of specific details:
- Named city, neighborhood, business
- Exact time + day of week
- Multi-brand outfit
- Object inventory of 8-12 items with brands
- 5+ skin imperfections
- Specific iPhone model + lighting source
- Detailed color grade

### Camera specs
Same as Style A — iPhone 15 Pro / 14 Pro, 24-26mm, eye-level or slightly off, no flash.

### What makes Style E different from Style A
Density of biographical detail. Where Style A says "in a Pilates studio at 11am", Style E says "in the changing-room corridor of a Reformer Pilates studio in West Hollywood at 11:05am on a Tuesday after a 9am class with Alyssa". The narrative texture is maxed out.

### Template approach

Use the Style A template but:
- Specify city + neighborhood + business name
- Add the day of the week if relevant ("Sunday at 4:25pm")
- Add what just happened ("after a 9am Pilates class", "fresh from the shower", "between meetings")
- Multiply outfit brands (3-4 brand mentions instead of 1-2)
- Multiply background objects (8-12 instead of 5)
- Multiply skin imperfections (5-7 instead of 3)
- Add color grade nuances (halation + over-sharpening + HDR + slight clipping)

### Example

```

Wearing a matte-black cropped long-sleeve Alo zip-up half-unzipped over a white Alo ribbed sports bra, matching high-waisted black Alo Airbrush leggings, white Adidas Sambas with bone-white crew socks, a tan leather Celine bucket bag slung on her left shoulder, a thin gold paperclip Mejuri necklace, a yellow gold Cartier Love bracelet on the right wrist, a single small gold Mejuri huggie in each ear.

## Choosing the right style — quick decision tree

Question: Is this a casual moment from her life captured on a phone?
- YES → **Style A** (default)
- NO → continue

Question: Is this a podcast / interview / "she's being filmed talking" moment?
- YES → **Style B**
- NO → continue

Question: Is this a posed editorial / magazine / "professional photographer" shot?
- YES → **Style C**
- NO → continue

Question: Is this a chaotic night / flash / party scene?
- YES → **Style D**
- NO → continue

Question: Does the user want maximum realism with full biographical narrative?
- YES → **Style E**
- NO → default back to **Style A**

---

## Mixing rules

Within a single prompt, NEVER mix:
- Style A (smartphone) with Style C (50mm full-frame) — physical impossibility
- Style B (cinematic warm) with Style D (raw flash) — contradictory looks
- "Studio lighting" with anything Style A/D — Style A and D are explicitly anti-studio

You CAN combine:
- Style A + lived-in imperfections from Style E (always)
- Style C with Style E narrative density (high-end editorial moments)
- Style B with Style E narrative density (specific podcast brand mentioned, etc.)
