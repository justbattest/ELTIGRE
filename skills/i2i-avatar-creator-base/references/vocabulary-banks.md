# Vocabulary Banks

Pull from these banks when filling in the universal template. The richer the specificity, the more realistic the output.

---

## 1. Camera Specs

### iPhone models (use specific model)
- iPhone 15 Pro front camera (selfies, mirror selfies)
- iPhone 15 Pro rear camera (third-person, taken by friend, mirror full-body)
- iPhone 14 Pro rear camera (slightly older look — slightly softer rendering)
- iPhone 13 Pro front camera (older selfie aesthetic, more visible noise)

### Focal lengths (smartphone equivalents)
- 24mm — ultra-wide selfie (front camera default), arms-length, slightly distorted
- 26mm — normal smartphone (rear default on iPhone 15 Pro)
- 28mm — slightly tighter rear smartphone
- AVOID: anything 35mm+ for Style A — that's full-frame camera territory

### Focal lengths (full-frame, for Styles B/C)
- 35mm — natural cinematic, wider scene
- 50mm — "human eye" feel, balanced portrait
- 85mm — tight portrait, max compression, max bokeh
- 24-70mm zoom at 50mm — versatile editorial

### Aperture
- f/1.8-f/2.0 — extreme shallow depth (Style C close-ups)
- f/2.2-f/2.8 — shallow with subject + immediate context (Style B)
- iPhone default aperture (f/1.8 on Pro, but rendered as smartphone, not pro)

### Perspective / angle
- Eye-level (default)
- Slightly below eye-level (confident, alluring, common for selfies)
- Slightly above eye-level (cute, casual, "phone held above")
- 3/4 profile (editorial close-up)
- Front-facing direct (mirror selfie, vlog)
- Side profile (rare, only for editorial)

### Distance phrasing
- Arm-length / arm extended (selfie)
- One-arm-extended slightly tilted (mirror selfie)
- Handheld at chest height (third-person taken by friend)
- A few steps ahead (third-person walking)
- Across the table (across-table candid)

### iPhone artifacts (CRITICAL — these add real-photo signal)
- "Slight fingerprint smear visible on the lower edge of the mirror"
- "Slight motion blur on the [hand / coffee cup / hair]"
- "Slight iPhone HDR halation around the brightest light"
- "Mild iPhone over-sharpening at the edges of [object]"
- "Very faint highlight clipping on the white [shirt/wall]"
- "Subtle digital noise in the shadows"
- "Barely-visible smudge on the lens edge"
- "Slight chromatic aberration on high-contrast edges"
- "iPhone-style soft falloff at the corners"

## 8. Phrases to ALWAYS use

These phrases consistently improve realism and should be on autopilot:

- "Natural skin texture fully preserved, no smoothing, no airbrushing"
- "Do not smooth the face. Do not soften the features."
- "Mid-[action]"
- "Specific time" + "specific location" + "specific outfit brand"
- "Visible pores"
- "Slight [sheen / shine / gloss / sweat / oil]"
- "Naturally [pink / red / flushed]"
- "Soft natural [smile / expression / pose]"
- "iPhone [model] [front/rear] camera, [focal] equivalent"

## 9. Phrases to NEVER use

These phrases trigger AI tells:
- "Beautiful", "stunning", "perfect", "flawless", "gorgeous"
- "Posed for the camera"
- "Looking at the camera with a smile" (without action)
- "Standing in front of"
- "Modern apartment / office / gym / café" (without inventory)
- "Studio lighting", "professional lighting" (for Style A)
- "8K resolution", "8K", "high resolution" (sounds like AI prompt vocabulary)
- "Hyper-detailed", "ultra-detailed" (paradoxically triggers AI smoothing)
- "Soft skin", "perfect skin", "smooth skin"
- "Symmetric", "balanced composition"
- "Photorealistic" alone (always pair with specifics)
- "Warm tungsten light" / "Edison bulb" / "candlelight" indoor — these often trigger the "AI yellow look" (prefer cool natural daylight or cool LED)
